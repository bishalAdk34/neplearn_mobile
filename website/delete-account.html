Delete your NepLearn account

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

This page explains how to delete your NepLearn account and the data associated with it. NepLearn is developed and operated by an independent developer.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@neplearn.com from the Google email address you use to sign in to NepLearn, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my NepLearn account.
  3. We will delete your account and data within 30 days and email you to confirm when it is complete.

An in-app "Delete account" button is not available yet. Until it is, email is the supported way to delete your account.

What gets deleted

When we process your request, we permanently delete the following from our servers:

  • Your account and sign-in record (name, email address, profile picture URL, user ID)
  • Profile preferences (display name, learning goal, skill level, daily goal, settings)
  • Learned words, XP, streaks, completed lessons and activity history
  • Spaced-repetition review schedules
  • Journal entries and AI feedback on them
  • AI Tutor chat history
  • Your entry on the weekly leaderboard

We do not keep any of this data after deletion is complete.

Data on your device

Some data (preferences, mistakes list, notification settings, and anything created in guest mode) is stored only on your phone. To remove it, uninstall NepLearn or clear the app's storage in your device settings. If you never signed in, there is nothing stored on our servers to delete.

Deleting only some of your data

You can also delete parts of your data without deleting your account:

  • Learned words — Settings → Clear Learned Words (removes them on your device and in the cloud).
  • AI chats — delete individual conversations from the AI Tutor screen.
  • Google access — revoke NepLearn's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

For other partial deletion requests, email support@neplearn.com.

See the Privacy Policy for full details on what we collect and why.
